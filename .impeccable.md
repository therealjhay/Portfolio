## Design Context

### Users
This portfolio serves a mixed audience: hiring managers/recruiters, potential freelance clients, and Web3 collaborators. Visitors arrive to quickly evaluate technical credibility and fit.

### Brand Personality
The interface should feel bold, credible, and technical, with a founder-led tone that communicates ownership and execution depth.

### Aesthetic Direction
High-signal technical presentation with confident visual emphasis, while preserving clarity and trust. The experience should feel modern and engineered rather than decorative.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with proof of capability through concrete projects and technical writing.
2. Balance strong visual identity with readability and straightforward navigation.
3. Prioritize trust signals (clarity, consistency, accessible interactions) over novelty.
4. Keep interactions purposeful and performance-conscious.
